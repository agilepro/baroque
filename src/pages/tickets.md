# 2027 Season Tickets

Experience the beauty, drama, and brilliance of Baroque music all season long! With season tickets to the Santa Cruz Baroque Festival, you’ll enjoy a curated lineup of captivating performances celebrating the music of the Baroque era. This year's season ticket is good for admission to the January fundraiser as well as the four series concerts. 

### Get access 

Tickets are available online at [CalTix](https://caltix.com/e/santa-cruz-baroque-festival/tickets). The January 23 fundraising concert is included in this year's season ticket.

Tickets may also be ordered by mail at P.O. Box 482, Santa Cruz, CA 
95061, or by phone at 831.457.9693.
